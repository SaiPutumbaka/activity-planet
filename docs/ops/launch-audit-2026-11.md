## Go-live checklist

**Ready (after merge)**

- Migration pipeline: staging first, then production on merge; production steps now in a `production` environment job, failed deploys fail the run.
- Moderator alerts for every new report and takedown, an hourly due sweep, a daily health check (needs C1).
- Email budget below the Resend free plan with a reserve for Auth email.
- Evidence hold on self-deletion; unfinished deletions retried and alerted.
- Acceptance of the Terms and Privacy Policy recorded; data download in the app.
- Shared-context DM rules, RSVP and read rules for moderated plans and removed hubs, banned hosts.
- Classifier spend capped per day and per sender.
- Fonts self-hosted; CSP Report-Only with the safe directives enforced; Permissions-Policy; hidden source maps; Turnstile fails closed.
- Everything already ready in the triage: RLS on every table, definer functions with `search_path`, storage policies, 18+ on the server, blocks and rate limits, no secrets in git, API budgets, realtime fallback, keepalive, no test data on production.

**Risky (accepted for launch, watch)**

- One-person moderation against the 4-hour, 24-hour and 48-hour targets until the second moderator (C1).
- CSP is Report-Only: watch the browser console on staging for a week, then enforce.
- Free-tier caps (runbook section 9); Supabase logs last one day.
- Vercel Hobby is non-commercial (FINANCIAL-PLAN trigger before any payment).
- Discovery gate on production starts red until it passes; EU discovery off.
- The backup workflow's Storage copy uses the CLI's experimental `storage cp`; watch its first run.
