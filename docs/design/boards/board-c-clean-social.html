Logo

A planet with company

The mark is a planet with a moon that has just arrived: two circles and one knockout, so it reads as "not alone" at a 24 px tab icon and a 1024 px App Store tile without being redrawn. The wordmark is drawn, not typed, as a lowercase geometric monoline whose bowls are the same circle as the planet, so the whole identity is one circle and one stroke weight.

Construction. Planet r 17, moon r 7.5, knockout r 10.5 on a 48 grid; the knockout is always moon + 3 so the gap scales with the mark. Wordmark: x-height 20, stroke 4.4, round caps, lowercase only, the circle of the a, p and e is the planet's circle. Clear space is one moon diameter. The mark is always one color (primary on light and dark, white on the icon tile, ink in print); never two colors, never a gradient.

Palette

Black, white, one blue

#1D5BFF is the only brand color on a screen. It is the one hue that stays fully saturated and still clears 4.5:1 with white text, so the Going button is identical in light and dark. Every ratio below is computed (WCAG 2.1, relative luminance); the first number is light, the second dark.

Type

One family, a weight ramp

Figtree for everything. Instagram and Snapchat ship on the system font and feel native because the type has no voice of its own beyond weight and size; Figtree gives us that with a warmer, rounder lowercase that matches the drawn wordmark. Tracking tightens as size grows. Numbers are tabular so times and counts line up in lists.

Motion

Fast, physical, quiet

Nothing floats, nothing glows. Motion confirms a touch or moves a thing from where it was to where it is now, in under 300 ms, on one easing curve. With reduced motion on, everything below becomes a 120 ms crossfade.

Press

Every tappable surface compresses to 97% on pointer-down and springs back on release. No color flash, no ripple; the compression is the feedback. Try any button on this board.

down 120 ms ease-out · up 180 ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)

RSVP success

The Going pill crossfades blue to green tint while the check draws itself left to right; your avatar slides into the stack from the right and the seat meter fills one more dot; the toast rises. One light haptic on the color change, not on the tap.

color 220 ms · check draw 260 ms ease-out · avatar in 240 ms · toast 200 ms rise from +12 px

Tab switch

No horizontal slide between tabs. The old screen fades under the new one while the tapped icon pops from 0.9 to 1.0 and its stroke thickens. Scroll position is kept per tab.

crossfade 150 ms ease-in-out · icon 200 ms cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1)

Pull to refresh

The mark sits above the feed. As you pull, the moon travels around the planet in step with your finger at 0.55 resistance; on release it finishes one orbit and the feed settles back with the new cards.

follow finger · release orbit 600 ms linear · settle 200 ms ease-out

Chat send

The bubble leaves the composer at 96% scale and rises into the thread; the composer clears on the same frame; a short tick haptic. Incoming messages fade in with no movement so a busy chat never jiggles.

send 180 ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) · receive 120 ms fade

Live dot

The only looping animation in the app: a ring expands from the Live dot and fades, slow enough to notice and never to distract. It stops under reduced motion.

ring 1.6 s cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) infinite · scale .5 → 1.25 · opacity .75 → 0
Why this is not AI slop

The choices, named

There is no gradient, no glow, no blur, no hero number with a tiny label; the only color on a screen is one blue pill and whatever the people are wearing. The blue is #1D5BFF because it is the single hue that stays fully saturated and still clears 4.5:1 with white text, which is why the Going button is pixel-identical in light and dark instead of being a "dark mode variant". Text is near-black on white with a three-step grey ramp that never drops below 4.8:1, not the foggy muted-foreground grey that makes generated mockups look unfinished. Cards get a hairline and a one-pixel shadow; dark mode is true black separated by borders, the way Instagram does it, not elevated grey slabs. The type is one family and a weight ramp with tracking that tightens as size grows; nothing is uppercase except the weekday on a date tile, and every number is tabular. State is encoded three ways at once (hue, icon, word) so a colorblind student still reads Full and Cancelled, and live is pink rather than red so it never looks like an error. The seat meter draws the product's actual quorum rule, "happens at 4", rather than decorating the card. The copy is the first market's own vocabulary: PIN, Student Center East Room 220, Freedom Park Trail, the Panthers game, Waffle House on Peachtree. The imported card keeps a different verb because the action genuinely leaves the app. And the logo is two circles and a knockout that reads as "not alone" at 24 px, drawn in the same stroke as the wordmark, so the identity is one idea rather than a mascot plus a font.
