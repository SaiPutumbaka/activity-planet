Logo

The Check-Orbit: a planet, and a check that doesn't stop at the edge.

At 24px, where it lives most of the time

Mark alone in the tab bar and on notifications; mark plus wordmark on the sign-in screen and share cards. The check's long stroke is 9 units on a 64 grid, so at 24px it is still a 3.4px line.

App icon

Default icon is near-black with the orange disc. The orange tile is the alternate icon offered in settings, and the only place the mark inverts.

The mark is a planet with a check whose long stroke leaves orbit, because the whole point of the app is that a tap turns into leaving the room. Inside the disc the check is ink, outside it takes the text color, so one drawing works on near-black, on white, and as a flat orange disc on a home screen, with no gradient anywhere.

Motion

Fast, physical, and quiet. Nothing moves on its own except the live dot. Everything respects reduced motion.

Press

Every tappable surface scales to .97 and darkens 4% on pointer-down, then releases with a touch of overshoot. No ripple, no color flash. The button feels like it has weight before anything else happens.

down 90ms ease-out up 160ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)

RSVP success

Tap Going: the pill crossfades Streetlight to the success fill while the check draws left to right. The newly lit seat in the row pops in with a small spring, the headcount rolls from 4 to 5, one medium haptic fires, and the Undo toast slides up. Reverse plays at half the duration.

fill 180ms ease-out check draw 200ms, 40ms delay seat spring 320ms (stiffness 380, damping 24) count roll 240ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)

Tab switch

Content crossfades, it never slides. The active icon fills its outline, the label weight stays the same so nothing jumps, and each tab keeps its own scroll position. Tapping the active tab scrolls to top.

crossfade 160ms linear icon fill 120ms ease-out scroll-to-top 300ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)

Pull to refresh

The feed follows the finger at half speed. At 72px the Check-Orbit mark fades in and its tail sweeps a full turn while loading, which is the one time the logo moves. On release the list snaps back and new cards settle in from 8px below.

resistance 0.5, threshold 72px tail spin 900ms linear, repeat snap back 260ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) new cards 200ms, stagger 30ms

Chat send

The input clears the instant you tap send. The bubble rises out of the input's position into the list; the delivered tick fades in once the server answers. The list stays pinned to the bottom, and incoming messages push up with the same curve.

bubble rise 200ms cubic-bezier(.2,.9,.3,1) tick fade 150ms ease-out incoming push 180ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)

Why this is not AI slop

Every dark UI a model draws right now has an acid-green or vermilion accent on charcoal. This one has Streetlight, an orange chosen because that's the color of downtown Atlanta at 9 p.m. and because the 2025 logo was orange too, and it is spent on exactly one job: committing inside the app. Text on it is ink, never white, because white on this orange is 2.6:1 and ink is 7.5:1, and that single rule makes the Going button look like Activity Planet instead of like a Shopify checkout. Leaving the app (Register on PIN) gets an inverse button, so the two kinds of yes never look alike.

Cards lead with the time in an expanded grotesk because a plan is a time before it is a title, and the seat row turns "4 of 6" into six marks you can read at arm's length. The near-black is neutral, not navy, and light mode is not an inversion: fills stay put and the text colors get their own darker values, each ratio computed and printed above. There are no gradients, no glows, no blur, no orbs; the only thing that animates unprompted is an 8-pixel live dot. Nine category colors each come with a drawn glyph so a hub never needs an emoji, the logo's check leaves orbit for a reason the product can say out loud, and the copy is written the way a GSU student talks: "Rec Center, 3rd floor tables", "running 5 late, start without me", "free pizza, apparently".
