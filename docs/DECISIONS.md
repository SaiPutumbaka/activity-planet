## D-031 — Account deletion is immediate, with a 30-day evidence ledger and no grace period (2026-10-20)
One RPC closes the account in a single transaction (tombstone profile, private data and memberships removed, handle released for 30 days, auth user banned) and an Edge Function frees the email by soft-deleting the auth user. Considered a 14- or 30-day grace period with a `cancel_account_deletion()` RPC; rejected because Apple 5.1.1(v) and Google Play accept a deferred deletion only when it is disclosed and short, nothing in the product yet justifies a cooling-off window, a restorable state keeps every piece of personal data for the whole window, and GoTrue cannot undo a soft delete. Someone who changes their mind signs up again with the same email. Retained: the `account_deletions` row (id, hashed email, timestamps, reason) for 30 days as moderation evidence, the released handle for 30 days, payment records as tax law requires, and a tombstone with no personal data so other people's content keeps a "Deleted user" author. The nightly run collects probation and enabled sources from `GET /collector/sources` and posts each one's result to `POST /collector/sources/record`; the probe itself (`GET`/`POST /collector/candidates`) is slice 8. Slice 8 (the discovery probe) is migration 47 and `packages/collector/src/discovery/`: candidates for a region come from the OSM `website` tags of event-likely places in its tiles (library, school, arts venue, community, attraction, maker, dance, cinema, sports centre, historic, nature, park, market; bars, breweries and nightlife second; never restaurants, shops, cafés or worship) and from Wikidata (P856 of the items those places link, and one 25 km `wikibase:around` query of event-type venues), one per host; the probe reads robots.txt, the homepage and up to 3 event pages found by path, host or link-text keywords in the region's languages (guessed paths only when nothing matched), at most 8 requests a host including robots.txt and redirect hops; it recognises The Events Calendar, Localist (always `needs_permission`), Trumba, CivicPlus, LibCal, Communico and Google Calendar embeds, and reads the best seed once through the collector's own pipeline. Cap: 150 probes a region a UTC day (`source_discovery_probe_queue`), 10 regions' candidates a night, 500 candidates a region. **Gate:** the probe may read candidates for any region only while the same probe code (a hash of the collector's `src/` and its discovery and denylist data) has passed, within 30 days, an Atlanta run from scratch that finds a calendar on at least 6 of the 9 Atlanta sources that work today; the database enforces it (`discovery_gate_closed`), and `discovery.yml` runs the gate as a check on every branch push and pull request that changes the collector. Measured Nov 4 (run 41927278244): 500 candidates (578 OSM hosts, 72 Wikidata entity sites, 108 around-query sites), 150 probed in 630 s with 899 requests to 218 hosts, 26 calendars found (23 with items inside the region, 3 Localist), and 6 of 9 rediscovered: Botanical Garden, Marietta, Piedmont Park, Atlanta History Center, Alliance Theatre (its `/offstage/` calendar) with items, Kennesaw State as Localist (`needs_permission`). ArtsATL, Park Pride and the contra dance society have no OSM place or Wikidata venue, so only a member suggestion can find them (design 6.1 item 3). EU, EEA and UK regions get no automatic candidates and no probes until the attorney review: the country list is in `packages/collector/data/discovery.json` (a test keeps it equal to `source_discovery_allowed`) and a region's country comes from its time zone, an unlisted European zone counting as gated. The first nightly staging run (41928904496) added 500 Atlanta candidates and probed 150: 18 went to probation, 120 were rejected, 2 need permission. Production discovery runs only from main with the repository variable `DISCOVERY_PRODUCTION` set to `true`. Slice 10 (lane 1 by region) is migration 48: Ticketmaster Discovery (`geoPoint` geohash and radius, every `locale`, no `countryCode` so a border inside the radius is not cut, test listings left out, prices in the listing's currency) and SeatGeek (`lat`/`lon`/`range`) run once per collector region a night, never for seeds-only city areas; their `feed_sources` rows (`ticketmaster-<region>`, `seatgeek-<region>`, lane `api`) are `listing_kind` `show`, so their events fill the "Shows and games" row and map layer. Keys: the repository secrets `TICKETMASTER_API_KEY` and `SEATGEEK_CLIENT_ID`, else Edge Function secrets of those names on the project the regions come from (`GET /collector/api-keys`, collector secret only, masked in the job); with neither, nothing is asked of either API and the lane is skipped. Budget: before collecting the job takes the run's calls (regions times the adapter's page cap: 5 pages of 200 for Ticketmaster, 10 of 100 for SeatGeek) from the day's budget in `collector_budget` (`collector_api_budget_take`, Ticketmaster 1,000 and SeatGeek 1,000 calls a UTC day; Ticketmaster's free key allows 5,000) and gives back what it did not send (`collector_api_budget_return`); a region the budget does not reach gets no batch that night (nothing of it counts as missed), one cut off mid-way and a query beyond the page cap are partial reads. The API hosts are limited by the budget, not the 60-requests-per-host cap; the 1 request per 2 s spacing stays (Ticketmaster's limit is 2 a second). Terms (read from search excerpts; the developer sites are blocked in Claude's environment): Ticketmaster allows caching only for reasonable periods and removal within 24 hours of an owner's request, and the listing links to its own event page to buy; SeatGeek wants its mark, linking to seatgeek.com, wherever its data appears (a "powered by" form is allowed). So shows link out as "Tickets on Ticketmaster" or "Tickets on SeatGeek", carry "Event data from Ticketmaster" or "Powered by SeatGeek" linking to the seller's homepage on cards, the event page, the row and the map list, never store images, and live only while refreshed: `collector_api_shows_sweep` (pg_cron daily) cancels a ticketing-only show not seen for 3 days as "No longer listed by the source." (it comes back when listed again) and clears the stored raw item a week after the show ends. Removal requests go to the existing support address and must be acted on within 24 hours for these listings. The SeatGeek logo itself and Ticketmaster's revenue clause stay with Sai (FOUNDER-TODO 20b and item 12).
Considered: Cloudflare R2 from day one (free egress, but a new account that probably asks for a card, and a DNS move or a Worker; kept as the scale-up step); Supabase Storage (egress shared with the database's 5 GB and would not fit at 10k monthly actives); keeping OSM places in Postgres (Germany alone, about 600k places at 1.0 to 1.6 KB a row, exceeds the whole 500 MB database); PMTiles or FlatGeobuf (the client clusters a few thousand points and filters by hub, so a small whole-tile file is cheaper); demand from map panning (an abuse vector and an interest leak); hand-written city seed files (do not scale past Atlanta); EU discovery from day one (database-right exposure before a legal read).
