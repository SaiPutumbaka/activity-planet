## D-031 — Account deletion is immediate, with a 30-day evidence ledger and no grace period (2026-10-20)
One RPC closes the account in a single transaction (tombstone profile, private data and memberships removed, handle released for 30 days, auth user banned) and an Edge Function frees the email by soft-deleting the auth user. Considered a 14- or 30-day grace period with a `cancel_account_deletion()` RPC; rejected because Apple 5.1.1(v) and Google Play accept a deferred deletion only when it is disclosed and short, nothing in the product yet justifies a cooling-off window, a restorable state keeps every piece of personal data for the whole window, and GoTrue cannot undo a soft delete. Retained: the `account_deletions` row (id, hashed email, timestamps, reason) for 30 days as moderation evidence, the released handle for 30 days, payment records as tax law requires, and a tombstone with no personal data so other people's content keeps a "Deleted user" author. ## D-036 — Location collection for Feed v2; Feed v2 stays in week 4 (Claude's call on Sai's feedback, 2026-10-31; Sai can reverse)
The walkthrough feed was empty because `feed_events` returns only events in hubs the viewer joined or tagged to a verified campus; it has no location input. Chose: a required onboarding step "Where do you hang out?" with an area picker (default Atlanta). "Use my location" is optional, only on tap, with a one-line explainer; coarse accuracy (`enableHighAccuracy: false`); the device point is sent with each feed request, rounded to about 1 km, and not stored. A saved home area lives on `profiles.home_area_id`, used for ranking and not shown publicly for now. Campus is one quick-pick for verified members, never the default. Never prompt for permission on load or during onboarding; after a denial, do not re-ask automatically (iOS can report "prompt" after a denial, so the app remembers the answer itself). Users outside the Atlanta areas get a "we're starting in Atlanta" state plus a city waitlist. Feed v2 adds a public-within-radius arm independent of hub membership (origin: the device point if given, else the home area, else Atlanta), widens the radius step by step before showing an empty state, and sorts human-hosted above imported. Public events with no point are included only from joined hubs or a verified campus. Approved-only (private address) events are treated as an approximate area, not a point, in distance filters, because their stored point can be several kilometres from the venue (D-038). Considered: a device-location prompt during onboarding (often denied, needs the area fallback anyway, iOS home-screen apps lose the permission state, and it invites storing the most sensitive data); campus as the default origin (wrong for anyone in Decatur or Buckhead); free-text city or ZIP geocoded by Geoapify (messy and uses quota; the waitlist covers other cities for now).

## D-038 — Map: MapLibre GL JS with OpenFreeMap tiles; private addresses never pinned (Claude's call on Sai's feedback, 2026-10-31; Sai can reverse)
Sai asked for a map of what is happening nearby. Chose MapLibre GL JS, lazy-loaded on the map route, with OpenFreeMap tiles: no key, no account; its FAQ allows commercial use with the OpenStreetMap/OpenMapTiles attribution on screen (https://openfreemap.org); it has no SLA and runs on donations. The style URL lives in `VITE_MAP_STYLE_URL` so the provider is swappable; the fallback is a self-hosted Protomaps PMTiles extract of Atlanta (https://github.com/protomaps/basemaps). MapTiler's free plan is non-commercial only (https://www.maptiler.com/terms/cloud/) and about 5,000 map sessions a month with service paused at the cap (https://www.maptiler.com/cloud/pricing/); Stadia's free tier is non-commercial too (https://stadiamaps.com/pricing/). Activity Planet plans revenue from coaching fees and sponsors, so MapTiler comes out of PLAN, FINANCIAL-PLAN (whose line also had the limit wrong) and the Privacy Policy processor list, replaced by OpenFreeMap, which receives viewers' IP addresses and the tiles they load. Terms were read through search excerpts because the hosts are blocked from the session; re-check once they are allowed (FOUNDER-TODO item 14).
