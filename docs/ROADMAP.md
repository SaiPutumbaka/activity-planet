# Roadmap: 2026-10-20 to the Atlanta launch, January 2027

Twelve weeks. Activity Planet is for everyone in Atlanta 18+ (D-035); the launch is timed to Georgia State's spring term because GSU is the first campus partnership and acquisition channel. Two tracks every week: **Build** (Claude does, Sai reviews) and **Business** (Sai does, Claude prepares drafts, lists and emails). GSU spring classes begin **Monday 2027-01-11**; drop/add runs Jan 11 to 15; MLK holiday Jan 18; most student orgs restart the week of Jan 19. GSU fall finals begin the week of Dec 7, and winter break follows, so student-facing asks happen before Nov 24 or after Jan 11.

Per D-013 option C, January launches as an installable web app; store submissions follow the evidence test in March. The two internal milestones stay: **release A on Dec 8** (events, chapters, messaging, trust and safety, deletion; no payments) and **release B by Jan 4** (coaching payments, recommendations, assistant), with a feature freeze for A on Dec 5, so the launch candidate is exercised for a month before the public launch.
