# Roadmap: 2026-10-20 to the January 2027 launch

Twelve weeks. Activity Planet is for adults 18+ anywhere (D-040, revised 2026-11-03; D-035 said Atlanta): each person sees events around their own location first, and the app collects events from every free, permitted source so it is useful the day someone signs up. Atlanta is the seeding city, where Sai recruits organizers and captains in person, and the fallback origin for people who give no location. College partnerships, Georgia State first, are optional tracks pursued only if they work out; nothing below waits on them. The public launch stays Monday Jan 11, which happens to be Georgia State's first day of spring classes and is useful if that partnership works out. Two tracks every week: **Build** (Claude does, Sai reviews) and **Business** (Sai does, Claude prepares drafts, lists and emails). For the optional GSU track: spring classes begin **Monday 2027-01-11**; drop/add runs Jan 11 to 15; MLK holiday Jan 18; most student orgs restart the week of Jan 19. GSU fall finals begin the week of Dec 7, and winter break follows, so student-facing asks happen before Nov 24 or after Jan 11.

Per D-013 option C, January launches as an installable web app; store submissions follow the evidence test in March. The two internal milestones stay: **release A on Dec 8** (the hub map, events, chapters, trust and safety, deletion; no messaging, no payments; D-041) and **release B by Jan 4** (messaging, the event collector, coaching payments, recommendations, assistant), with a feature freeze for A on Dec 5, so the launch candidate is exercised for a month before the public launch.
